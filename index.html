Call Transcriber

Transcribes calls that mix English and Egyptian Arabic, using your own Gemini API key. The key and everything else stays in this browser — nothing goes anywhere except Google's API.

Advanced settings

Supported audio: mp3, wav, m4a/aac, ogg, flac, webm. Very long recordings may take a couple of minutes to process. If a step fails, it automatically retries with a simpler method for files under ~14 MB.
